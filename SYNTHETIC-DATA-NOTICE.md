This repository is a development and presentation demonstration. It is not approved for real healthcare or other sensitive records and does not provide medical, legal, benefits, disability, eligibility, or other professional advice or determinations. It is not an official SSA form or submission and makes no production, security, clinical, legal, or HIPAA-compliance claim.

The optional OpenAI feature is limited to the included synthetic sources. OpenAI output enters an unreviewed proposal queue and cannot alter accepted case state until a human explicitly accepts or applies a bounded edit. The demonstrated packet is generated deterministically from reviewed accepted state.
