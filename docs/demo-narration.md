# Build for Good demo narration

Voice production baseline: Kokoro 0.9.4, `am_fenrir`, American English, speed 0.66, mono 48 kHz PCM-16. Disposable warm-up speech is removed from each inference start before assembly.

## 01 — The problem

Disability and complex healthcare cases can scatter one person's story across providers, portals, tests, forms, and personal records. The challenge is not merely storing documents. It is preserving what the evidence says, what remains uncertain, and what a human has actually accepted.

## 02 — Neverlost's approach

Neverlost begins with a simple rule: preserve before you understand. Case Navigator keeps each canonical source visible before interpretation. Open A I can surface candidate information, but every result remains a proposal. A human decides whether it becomes part of the accepted case. A I proposes. Human decides.

## 03 — Synthetic source to OpenAI proposals

This demonstration follows Maya, a fully synthetic client. We open her cardiology and orthostatic evaluation, source S zero two. For repeatable judging, this recording replays the outputs from our validated live Open A I smoke test. The server reconstructs the canonical fixture, and local validation requires exact preserved evidence before any candidate enters review.

## 04 — Human review and accepted state

Candidates arrive marked as A I generated and unreviewed. One reflects findings consistent with POTS. Another preserves missing autonomic testing. Neither changes Maya's case on arrival. The consultant checks evidence, then can accept, edit, reject, or hold. Here, supported evidence is accepted, an unsupported claim rejected, and unresolved evidence held. Only reviewed content enters accepted state and the timeline.

## 05 — Packet and provenance

From reviewed state, Case Navigator generates a disability-focused packet through its deterministic output path. It is not drafted directly from arbitrary A I text. Supported information appears with source context; rejected or held material does not silently reappear. Provenance traces an accepted statement through its source, proposal, human decision, timeline, and packet appearances.

## 06 — Safety and vision

Everything shown uses synthetic data. It is not medical or legal advice, is not approved for production healthcare data, and makes no HIPAA or benefits determination. Production security and compliance require separate architecture. Neverlost helps people and systems work with complex information without losing context, provenance, or human responsibility.
