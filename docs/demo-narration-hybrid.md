# Build for Good hybrid demo narration

Voice production: Kokoro 0.9.4, `am_fenrir`, American English, speed 0.72, mono 48 kHz PCM-16. The spoken source deliberately uses `A. I.` and lowercase `pots`; captions restore the display forms `AI` and `POTS`.

## 01 — The problem

Disability and complex healthcare cases scatter one person's story across providers, portals, tests, forms, and personal records. The problem is preserving what the evidence actually says.

## 02 — Preserve before you understand

Neverlost starts with one rule: preserve before you understand. The canonical source stays visible. A. I. can surface candidates, but each remains a proposal. A human decides what enters the case.

## 03 — Validated OpenAI replay

This synthetic demonstration follows Maya. We open source S zero two: her cardiology and orthostatic evaluation. In the validated Open A. I. workflow, the server reconstructs the source and returns candidates. For repeatable judging, this sequence replays live-validated output. It makes no paid request.

## 04 — Unreviewed evidence

Candidates return A. I. generated and unreviewed, each with its supporting excerpt. One reflects findings consistent with pots. Another preserves missing autonomic testing.

## 05 — The human gate

Nothing changes Maya's case on arrival. The consultant checks the evidence, then chooses accept, bounded edit, reject, or hold. A. I. proposes. Human decides.

## 06 — Accepted state

Only an explicit human decision creates accepted case state. The supported pots statement enters Maya's timeline. Rejected and held information does not.

## 07 — Deterministic packet

From reviewed state, Case Navigator generates a disability-focused packet deterministically. The packet uses accepted statements, not arbitrary A. I. text.

## 08 — Provenance

Provenance traces the accepted statement through source, proposal, decision, timeline, and packet appearances. The evidence chain stays visible.

## 09 — Rejected evidence stays excluded

Rejected evidence remains traceable, but it has no accepted statement, no timeline appearance, and no output appearance. Governance prevents quiet re-entry.

## 10 — Safety and vision

Everything shown is synthetic. This is not medical or legal advice, is not approved for production healthcare data, and makes no HIPAA or benefits determination. Neverlost preserves context, provenance, and human responsibility.
